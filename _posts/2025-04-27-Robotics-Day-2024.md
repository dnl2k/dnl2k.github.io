In celebration of National Robotics Week, we welcomed Buffalo locals to explore our latest robotics advancements. The event featured K–12 activities, research talks, and live demonstrations—including legged robots, humanoid robots, nanobots, autonomous vehicles, and UAVs. It was heartwarming to see the sparkle of curiosity and excitement in the eyes of both children and adults as they interacted with the robots. I had the opportunity to present my research to the general public. Although I couldn’t demo my work this year, I’m already planning to prepare a proper demonstration for next year.
